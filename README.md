# Masar · مسار

**Find the businesses that are ready to buy.**

Masar maps the market across the Gulf and the Middle East, from Amman to the GCC, and brings back
the companies worth talking to, with the details needed to reach them.

## How it works

1. **Tell Masar who you sell to.** Pick the industries, cities and the kind of customer you want.
2. **Masar goes looking.** It reads the public web and maps, checks that each business is real and
   active, and finds the right way to reach it.
3. **You get a ranked list.** Every lead comes with a fit score and a chance of closing, so you start
   with the ones most likely to say yes.
4. **Follow up and track.** Keep your lists, goals and progress in one dashboard, in Arabic or English,
   on the web, your computer or your phone.

The rest stays with us.
